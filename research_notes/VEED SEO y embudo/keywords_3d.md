## Q1. Volume (US + global), KD, CPC and parent topic for the core clusters, expanded with matching, related and question terms

### Takeaway
Demand is concentrated in the generic "image/photo to 3D" head terms. "image to 3d model" has 6.9k US and 32k global searches, KD 51. "ai 3d model generator" has 8.7k US and 28k global, KD 69. These head terms are hard. The furniture, Shopify, AR and USDZ terms are small (20–600 US/mo each) but have **KD 0–10 and high CPCs ($2–$40)**, so they convert better and are easy to win. Several keywords from the brief have zero measurable volume: "add 3d model to shopify", "3d model from photo free" (US), "how to make a 3d model of furniture from a photo", "ar view furniture" and "3d model dimensions".

### Inferences
- The brief's exact phrases ("add 3d model to shopify", "how to make a 3d model of furniture from a photo") have no measurable volume. Searchers use generic "image to 3d" language plus platform words ("shopify ar", "woocommerce 3d product viewer"). Furniture-specific phrasing therefore needs to be built as **long-tail modifiers on generic tool pages**, not as standalone head targets.
- The high-CPC ecommerce terms are where buyer intent sits: "3d for ecommerce" $40, "3d product configurator ecommerce" $35, "3d viewer for website" $19, "3d product rendering services" $15, "3d furniture configurator" $12. - Global volume for converter and viewer terms is 5–10× US volume (e.g. glb viewer 1.7k US vs 16k global). Free utilities are an international traffic play and mostly attract creators and developers, not merchants.

### Takeaway
"image to 3d model" and "2d image to 3d model" are pure **tool SERPs** dominated by AI 3D generators (DR 46–77). A DR-34 exact-match domain still ranks #8, so the space is contestable with a strong tool page. The converter SERPs ("glb to usdz") are won by small utility sites (DR 39–49) and are the easiest wins. "shopify 3d models" is a **docs/blog SERP** held by Shopify Help (DR 97) but with KD 9. "ar furniture" is an **AR-15 gun-parts SERP**; avoid it. An AI Overview appears on nearly every target SERP.

## Q3. Top pages and organic keywords of tripo3d.ai, meshy.ai, 3daistudio.com, kaedim3d.com and modelry.ai: which subfolders and page types bring traffic

### Takeaway
Meshy and Tripo get most non-brand traffic from three page types:
1. a **/features/image-to-3d** tool landing page;
2. a **free 3D model gallery/library** (Meshy /free-3d-models, about 29k/mo; Tripo /3d-model-gallery/{animal});
3. a **programmatic /3d-tools/ subfolder** of format converters and online viewers (/file-converter/{a}/to/{b}, /online-viewer/{fmt}).

3D AI Studio's biggest non-home page is a **competitor comparison page** (/ai-3d-generator-comparison/meshy, about 6.8k/mo, ranking on "meshy ai"). Kaedim (about 1.4k/mo) and Modelry (about 400/mo) get almost no organic traffic, so the B2B ecommerce-3D niche is under-served in SEO.

### Cited Findings

**tripo3d.ai** (top pages by estimated monthly organic traffic, all countries) — [ASE](https://app.ahrefs.com/site-explorer)
- Home: 30,273 (brand "tripo ai" 19k).
